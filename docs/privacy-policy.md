AWARE helps you sort your waste and share what you make from it. This policy
explains what the app stores, why, and how to delete it. AWARE is made by
[Nguyen Truong Son]. Contact: [email address].

## The short version

- Scanning happens on your phone. Camera images are never uploaded.
- You don't need to give your name, email or phone number. The app starts with
  a guest account.
- What you post in the Gallery (text, photos and, only if you choose, a rough
  location) is visible to everyone using AWARE.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and your data is never sold.
- You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

## What AWARE stores and why

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A random account ID | When you first open the app | To keep your scans, points and posts together |
| Display name and avatar colours | Given automatically ("Recycler 1234"); you can change the name | Shown on your posts, replies and the leaderboard |
| Scan results: the item type, your answer to the sorting question, the time and the points | Each time you scan and add the result | Points, streaks, your stats and the leaderboard |
| Gallery posts and replies: text, photos, material tag | When you post or reply | To share them with the community |
| A location rounded to about 500 m | Only when you turn on "On the map" for a post | To show the post on the recycling map |
| Likes, saves, reports and blocks | When you use them | To run the Gallery and keep it safe |
| When you accepted the community rules | Before your first post or reply | The App Store requires community rules |
| A push notification token | Only if you turn on notifications | To tell you about replies, likes and announcements |
| An Apple ID identifier | Only if you choose Sign in with Apple | So you can get your account back on a new phone. AWARE does not ask Apple for your name or email |

The recycling map can use your phone's location to show nearby projects. That
location stays on your phone unless you attach it (rounded) to a post.

Streak and goal reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent to us.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (a database and file storage service) in its
Singapore region. Push notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification
service. [Add any other service used.]

## Who can see it

- **Everyone using AWARE:** your display name, avatar, points and rank, and
  your posts, replies and their photos. Photos are stored at web addresses that
  anyone with the address can open.
- **AWARE's moderators:** reports and the content they point to, to keep the
  community safe. Moderators can hide or remove content and suspend accounts
  that break the community rules.
- Nobody else. We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Deleting a post removes it and its photos. Deleting your
account (More → Account → Delete account) removes your account, scans, points,
posts, replies, photos, likes, saves, reports and blocks. Copies in backups
disappear within [30] days.

## Your choices and rights

- Change your display name in More → Account.
- Turn notifications off with the switch in More.
- Turn location off in the iPhone Settings app; the map still works.
- Delete your account in More → Account.
- Contact [email address] to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.
