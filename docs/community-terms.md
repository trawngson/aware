The AWARE Gallery is a friendly place to share what you made from things you
recycled. By posting or replying, you agree to these terms.

## The rules

1. **Be kind.** No bullying, hate, harassment or threats.
2. **Keep it about recycling**, upcycling and caring for the planet.
3. **Only share photos you took**, and never anyone's personal details
   (addresses, phone numbers, faces of people who didn't agree).
4. **No ads or spam.**
5. **Nothing sexual, violent, illegal or dangerous.**

AWARE has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

## What happens when rules are broken

- Anyone can report a post, reply or person. When [3] people report the same
  post or reply, it is hidden until a moderator looks at it.
- A basic word filter stops some offensive words in English and Vietnamese.
- Moderators review reports [within 24 hours] and can remove content and
  suspend people who keep breaking the rules. Suspended people can't post,
  reply or like.
- You can block anyone. You won't see their posts or replies, and they won't
  notify you.

## Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let AWARE show it in the
app to other users. You can delete your posts and replies at any time, and
deleting your account deletes all of them.
